Technology stack

Four engineered pathways from API crystal to clinical dose.

Particle size is not a finishing step. It is a formulation decision that dictates dissolution rate, bioavailability, deposition profile, and shelf stability. We choose the pathway from the target, not the equipment.

01 / JET MILLING

Spiral jet micronisation

Compressed nitrogen at 7 bar accelerates particles through a venturi into a counter-rotating chamber. Inter-particle collisions reduce size to 1–10 μm without heat input — critical for thermolabile APIs and high-potency compounds. Output is captured in a cyclone and graded by cascade impactor.

Target range1–10 μm D50
Throughput5–80 kg/h
02 / BEAD MILLING

Wet nanomilling

A rotor-stator agitates yttria-stabilised zirconia beads against a drug suspension, shearing crystals to 100–500 nm. The result is a stable nanocrystal dispersion — bioavailability gains of 4× to 12× over micronised material for BCS Class II compounds.

Target range100–500 nm
Batch size2–400 L
03 / SPRAY DRYING

Engineered amorphous dispersions

API and polymer excipient are co-dissolved and atomised into a heated chamber. Solvent evaporation is faster than crystal nucleation, locking the API into an amorphous solid dispersion. Used for poorly soluble molecules and inhaled powder platforms.

Target range1–5 μm MMAD
Solvents11 approved
04 / SUPERCRITICAL

RESS & SAS crystallisation

Supercritical CO₂ at 31 °C and 74 bar acts as both solvent and antisolvent. Particles nucleate and grow under tight thermodynamic control, producing uniform polymorphs with no residual solvent — the cleanest route available for respiratory and parenteral APIs.

Target range0.5–3 μm
Pressureup to 300 bar

How we work

A four-stage path from feasibility to commercial supply.

01
Feasibility

10–100 g samples are processed across candidate pathways. PSD, polymorph, and yield are measured. A target specification is drafted jointly with the client's formulation team.

2–4 weeks
02
Optimisation

Design-of-experiments over pressure, feed rate, nozzle geometry, and solvent composition. The optimal setpoint is locked and a process parameter envelope is defined.

6–10 weeks
03
Validation

Three consecutive GMP batches at pilot scale. Full analytical release, stability initiation (ICH Q1A), and CMC documentation package prepared for regulatory filing.

3–4 months
04
Commercial

Scale to commercial line. Annual capacity reservation, dual-site redundancy, and continuing stability monitoring under a quality agreement.
